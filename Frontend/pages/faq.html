Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about browsing products, managing your account, placing orders, shipping and getting support from Kokobeads.

Products and Shopping

Kokobeads offers beads, bracelets, anklets, necklaces and other accessories listed in the online store. Visit the Shop or Collections page to explore currently available products.

Browse the shop and collections, or use the search and filtering features available on the website. Check the product page for its displayed description, price and availability.

Product photographs are intended to help you understand what is offered. Colours can appear slightly different depending on lighting and your device's screen settings. Review the product description before ordering.

Accounts and Orders

Some store features, including account-related functions and viewing your order history, require you to sign in. You can visit the Login page or create an account.

Sign in to your account and open My Orders to view the orders associated with your account. You must be signed in to access your order history.

Visit the Forgot Password page and follow the available instructions to request a password reset.

Contact Kokobeads as soon as possible with your order reference. Whether cancellation is possible depends on the order's status and applicable requirements. Read our Returns & Refunds information for further guidance.

Shipping and Returns

Visit our Shipping Information page for the shipping details published by Kokobeads. Check the information relevant to your order before completing checkout.

Contact us with your order reference and a description of the issue. If possible, include photographs of the damaged or incorrect item. See the Returns & Refunds page for guidance.

Contact Kokobeads with your order details and explain why you are requesting a refund. Requests are reviewed according to the circumstances, the applicable policy and legal requirements. Read our Returns & Refunds policy.

Privacy and Support

Read our Privacy Policy for information about the collection, use, storage and handling of personal information when using the website.

Visit the Contact page and use the contact options provided there. Include relevant order details when asking about a purchase.

Still Have Questions?

If you cannot find the answer you need, contact Kokobeads and explain how we can help.
