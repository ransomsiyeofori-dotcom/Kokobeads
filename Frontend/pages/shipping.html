Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about how we prepare, ship, and deliver your Kokobeads order.

Order Processing

Once your order is confirmed, our team prepares your pieces carefully for delivery.

  • Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.
  • Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.
  • You will receive delivery updates when tracking information is available.

Delivery Times

  • Port Harcourt deliveries generally take 1–3 business days after processing.
  • Deliveries to other locations in Nigeria generally take 2–7 business days.
  • Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on the courier and destination.

Delivery Address

Please make sure your delivery information is complete and accurate when placing your order.

Kokobeads cannot be responsible for delivery problems caused by an incomplete or incorrect address supplied at checkout.

Delivery Delays

Occasionally, deliveries may take longer than expected because of courier delays, public holidays, severe weather, traffic, or other circumstances outside our control.

If your order appears to be significantly delayed, please contact us and we will help you check its delivery status.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fees depend on the delivery destination and will be displayed during checkout before you complete your order.
