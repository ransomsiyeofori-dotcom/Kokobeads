Returns & Refunds

Need help with an order? Find out what information to provide when contacting Kokobeads about a return, damaged item, incorrect product or refund enquiry.

1. Contact Us About an Order

If something is wrong with your order, contact Kokobeads so we can review the situation and explain the available next steps.

When contacting us, please provide:

  • Your name and the email address associated with the order.
  • Your order reference, if one is available.
  • The name of the product concerned.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Photographs, where relevant and available, showing damage or an incorrect item.
Contact Kokobeads

2. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If an item arrives damaged or differs from the product you ordered, please contact us and describe the problem. Photographs can help us understand the issue.

We will review the information provided and communicate the appropriate next steps in line with applicable law and the circumstances of the order.

3. Return Requests

If you wish to return an item, contact Kokobeads before sending it back. Please explain why you want to return the item and provide your order details.

We will advise you about the applicable return process after reviewing your request. Do not assume that a return has been approved until you receive confirmation.

Important: Return eligibility, applicable deadlines, item-condition requirements and return arrangements must be confirmed with Kokobeads. Any rights you have under applicable consumer-protection law remain unaffected.

4. Refund Enquiries

If you believe a refund may be due, contact us with your order reference and an explanation of the situation. We will review the request and communicate the outcome and any applicable next steps.

Refund approval, the amount and processing arrangements depend on the circumstances, the applicable policy and any legal requirements. This page does not guarantee automatic refunds for every request.

5. Order Cancellation

If you want to cancel an order, contact Kokobeads as soon as possible and include your order reference.

Whether an order can be cancelled depends on its current status and the applicable requirements. We will review the request and let you know what can be done.

6. Shipping and Delivery Problems

For questions about delivery, shipping information or an order that has not arrived as expected, please contact us with your order details.

Shipping Information

7. Need More Help?

Our contact page is the starting point for questions about orders, returns and refunds. Include enough information for us to identify the order and understand your concern.
