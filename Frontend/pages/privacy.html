Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information Kokobeads may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Kokobeads. It may include the following categories:

  • Account information: your name, email address, account details, and information needed to manage your account.
  • Order information: products ordered, quantities, prices, order totals, and order or payment status.
  • Delivery information: recipient name, phone number, delivery address, city, state, and country.
  • Authentication information: information used to sign in, verify an account, or process a password-reset request.
  • Shopping preferences: information stored by your browser to support shopping cart and wishlist functionality.
  • Communications: information you provide when contacting us about an order, account, or other enquiry.

Please do not send us payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive information through ordinary email or contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing orders and maintaining order records.
  • Arranging delivery and communicating about orders.
  • Responding to questions, complaints, and support requests.
  • Supporting sign-in, account verification, and password resets.
  • Maintaining the shopping cart, wishlist, and essential website functions.
  • Protecting the website, preventing misuse, and investigating suspected fraud.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations.

We will not use your personal information for a purpose that is incompatible with the reason it was collected unless we have a lawful basis to do so.

3. Orders, Payments, and Delivery

When you place an order, we use relevant account, product, order, and delivery information to record the purchase and arrange fulfilment.

Order records may include product names, quantities, prices, totals, order status, payment status, and timestamps. Delivery information may include the recipient's name, phone number, and delivery address.

Payment status may be recorded as pending, paid, failed, or refunded. The information available to Kokobeads depends on the payment method and the systems actually used to process the transaction.

Do not assume that submitting an order means payment has been completed. Follow the payment instructions shown during checkout and retain your order confirmation.

4. Accounts, Google Sign-In, and Password Resets

If you create an account, we use your account information to identify you and provide account-related features.

Kokobeads supports Google sign-in. If you choose this option, information provided through Google's sign-in process may be used to authenticate you and associate your sign-in with your account.

If you request a password reset, we may use your email address to send instructions for resetting your password. Reset links may expire for security reasons.

Keep your login details confidential. If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, contact us promptly.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

Kokobeads uses browser storage, including local storage, to support website functionality. For example, cart and wishlist information may be stored in your browser so those features can work between page visits.

Depending on the authentication flow and configuration, cookies or similar technologies may also be used to support sign-in and maintain an authenticated session.

Browser storage is associated with the browser and device you use. Clearing site data or using a different browser may remove locally stored shopping information or change how website features behave.

You can manage browser storage and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or clearing them may affect some features of the website.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information as part of our ordinary business operations. We may share information where necessary and appropriate in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: with providers that support website hosting, account authentication, email delivery, or other essential business functions.
  • Delivery and fulfilment: with delivery providers where necessary to deliver your order.
  • Legal obligations: where disclosure is required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a competent authority.
  • Security and protection: where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, or safeguard our rights and systems.

We aim to limit information shared with service providers to what is reasonably necessary for their assigned functions. Their handling of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and legal duties.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password where applicable and avoid sharing your account credentials.

If you suspect a security issue involving your account or personal information, please contact us so we can investigate.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, order fulfilment, customer support, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal or record-keeping requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and why it is needed. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of certain processing, or other available privacy remedies.

You may also be able to update certain account details through your account or contact us for assistance. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as resolving disputes and maintaining transaction records.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details on our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests.

10. Children's Privacy

Kokobeads is not intended to collect personal information from children in circumstances where doing so would violate applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our website, business practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so you understand how your information is handled.

12. Contact Kokobeads About Privacy

If you have questions about this policy, how your information is used, or a privacy request, please contact us through our official contact page.

Privacy enquiries: Contact Kokobeads and include enough information for us to understand your request. Please do not include your password or other unnecessary sensitive information.
